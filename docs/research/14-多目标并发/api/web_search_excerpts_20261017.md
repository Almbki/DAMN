## O2. Schmidt & DeShon 2007 — 动态目标优先级

- **PubMed 17638455** — 摘要原文（核心）："goal-performance discrepancies were significantly related to subsequent time allocation. Greater distance from a given goal resulted in greater time subsequently allocated to that goal. ... When the incentives for each goal were equivalent, ... greater time allocated to whichever goal was furthest from completion. However, with an incentive available for only 1 of the 2 goals, time allocation was largely determined by progress toward the rewarded goal. ... avoidance-framed goal was a stronger predictor ... the influence of goal-performance discrepancies differed as a function of the time remaining for goal pursuit."
- **APA PsycInfo / PsycArticles** — 同摘要，Citation: Schmidt, A. M., & DeShon, R. P. (2007). JAP 92(4):928–941.
- **SMU Lee thesis (2023)** — 复述 MGPM 的相位预测："participants were more likely to prioritize the goal further from completion when they had sufficient resources (e.g., time) ... but switch to prioritize the goal closest to the aspiration level when the time available starts to run out (Schmidt, Dolis, & Tolli, 2009)." → 印证 [4] MGPM 与 [2] 的相位切换。
- **Schmidt & Dolis 2009 PDF (goal-lab.psych.umn.edu)** — "Something's Got to Give: The Effects of Dual-Goal Difficulty, Goal Progress, and Expectancies on Resource Allocation" JAP 94(3):678-691. 引用 Kernan & Lord 1990 的相反发现（"participants allocated more time toward whichever of two goals they were making better progress toward"），揭示文献冲突（未做卡，但 [2] 卡片中标注此冲突已被 [4] MGPM 调和）。

## O3. Steel & König 2006 — 时间动机理论 TMT

- **MIT OCW PDF** — 摘要原文："we construct a temporal motivational theory (TMT). TMT appears consistent with the major findings from many other investigations, including psychobiology and behaviorism." 公式："motivation can be understood by the effects of expectancy and value, weakened by delay, with differences for rewards and losses."
- **Frontiers in Psychology 2018, 10.3389/fpsyg.2018.00327** — 引用 TMT 简化公式：`Motivation = Expectancy × Value / (1 + Impulsiveness × Delay)`；"motivation increases hyperbolically as the time to the deadline draws near."
- **Academy of Management 10.5465/amr.2006.22527462** — 出版方页面，确认 AMR 31(4):889-913。
